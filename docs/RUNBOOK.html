1 · Your first debate 3 min

  1. Double-click START-DEBATE. A page opens in your browser.
  2. Look at 1 · Is everything ready? You want green dots on Kilo · free, Voices and Speakers.
  3. Press the big Start at the top.
You should see: “Preparing: 1 of 2 voices ready · sound starts in about N s”. After about 20–40 seconds David speaks, then Zira answers, turn after turn, with a short silence between them. The counters show written running ahead of heard.
If not: see the card below that matches what you see.

Source: Open the debate page (after START-DEBATE)

2 · “Preparing…” and nothing plays 2 min

  1. Wait: it starts by itself after at most 90 seconds (the page shows the countdown).
  2. If a red What went wrong line appears: pick another model for that performer (the fastest today was nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free), then press Start again.
Why: free models are sometimes slow or busy. The debate never swaps the model behind your back; it tells you.

Source: Debate page · section 3 “Now”

3 · Voices show “not running” 1 min

  1. Press Start voices on the page. It starts the Line Machine in the background (no window, no sound).
  2. Wait about a minute; the dot turns green by itself.
Still red? Tell Jimmy “debate voices won’t start”.

Source: Debate page · section 1 “Is everything ready?”

4 · A source says “needs key” or “offline” 0 min

Nothing to do now: Kilo · free works without a key. OpenCode Zen and Cline free lanes need the 9router key; LM Studio and Ollama appear by themselves when you open them.

Source: The list of model sources

5 · Pause and Stop 2 min

  1. While someone speaks, press Pause: the voice should freeze mid-word, and nothing new is written.
  2. Press Resume: it continues from the same word.
  3. Press Stop: silence at once. Turns written ahead are never played; they are kept in the session folder.
If the sound did not freeze or stop: press Stop again, then Stop server, and tell Jimmy. This is the one check no agent has heard yet (agents may not play sound without your permission).

Source: The pending listening checks
